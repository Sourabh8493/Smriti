# Market item images

Photographs of everyday items bought at a north-eastern market. Shown on the
intro screen (the shopping list) and in the recall question.

## Naming

```
<level>_<name>.png
```

`<level>` is the lowest level the item appears at — an item marked `1` also
appears at levels 2 and 3. `<name>` uses dashes and becomes the label.

```
1_rice.png
1_fish.png
1_banana.png
1_eggs.png
1_tea-leaves.png
1_pumpkin.png
2_mustard-oil.png
2_chilli.png
2_ginger.png
2_jaggery.png
3_bamboo-shoot.png
3_betel-nut.png
```

Accepted extensions: `.png` `.webp` `.jpg`

## What makes a good image here

- **Transparent background PNG**, or a plain white one. The item sits on a
  card, so a busy background reads as clutter.
- **One item, centred, square crop.** Not a pile, not a market stall.
- **Instantly nameable.** This is a memory task, not an identification task —
  if a patient has to work out what they are looking at, the memory
  measurement is contaminated.
- **Around 600×600px** is plenty.
- **Consistent look across the set.** Mixing cut-outs with photos taken on a
  wooden table makes some options stand out for the wrong reason, which the
  patient can exploit without remembering anything.
